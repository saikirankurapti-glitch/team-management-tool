# Administrator Guide: Enterprise Governance & Custom Workflows

## 1. Enterprise Governance Center (`/settings/governance`)
Access workflow configuration, custom fields, approval sequences, organization policies, and configuration health reports.

## 2. Configuring Custom Workflows & Transitions
- Define custom statuses and categories (*BACKLOG*, *UNSTARTED*, *IN_PROGRESS*, *COMPLETED*, *ARCHIVED*).
- Configure transition rules with allowed roles and required fields.
- Assign custom workflows to specific projects.
