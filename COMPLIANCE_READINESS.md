# Enterprise Compliance Readiness Specifications (SOC 2 / GDPR)

## Compliance Controls Matrix
- **Tenant Isolation**: Server-side pre-retrieval authorization.
- **Audit Trails**: Immutable `AuditLog`, `AiAuditLog`, and `WebhookDeliveryLog` history.
- **Data Minimization**: Personal data minimized in external integrations and AI payloads.
