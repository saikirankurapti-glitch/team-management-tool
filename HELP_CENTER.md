# Help Center & In-App Documentation Specifications

## Help Center Categories (`/docs` or `HelpCenterModal.tsx`)
- **Getting Started**: Workspace setup & first project template.
- **Work Items & Boards**: Epics, Stories, Bugs, Kanban WIP limits.
- **Sprint Management**: Sprint planning & velocity forecasting.
- **Integrations & AI**: GitHub webhooks & AI Copilot prompts.
- **Billing & Security**: Plans, metered limits & SSO configuration.
